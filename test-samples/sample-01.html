Numerical

Numerical reasoning

Data and arithmetic under time pressure — percentages, ratios, rates. One objectively correct answer.

num-001

A company's annual revenue was £2.4 million in 2022 and rose to £3.0 million in 2023. What was the percentage increase in revenue?

  • A20%
  • B25%✓ correct
  • C30%
  • D60%
Why B. The increase is £0.6m on a base of £2.4m. 0.6 / 2.4 = 0.25 = 25%. (30% divides by the new figure; 20% and 60% are common miscalculations.)
num-004

A product is priced at £80. The price is first increased by 15%, then the new price is reduced by 15%. What is the final price?

  • A£78.20✓ correct
  • B£80.00
  • C£81.20
  • D£82.80
Why A. 80 × 1.15 = 92, then 92 × 0.85 = 78.20. A percentage rise followed by the same percentage fall does not return to the original — it ends lower.
num-010

8 workers build a wall in 6 days. Working at the same rate, how long would 12 workers take to build the same wall?

  • A3 days
  • B4 days✓ correct
  • C5 days
  • D9 days
Why B. The job is 8 × 6 = 48 worker-days. With 12 workers: 48 / 12 = 4 days.
num-023

£120 is shared between A and B in the ratio 2 : 3. How much does B receive?

  • A£48
  • B£60
  • C£72✓ correct
  • D£80
Why C. There are 5 parts. 1 part = 120 / 5 = £24. B gets 3 parts = £72.
Verbal

Verbal reasoning

Read the passage, then judge the statement: True (follows from the passage), False (contradicts it), or Cannot Say (undetermined). The three sample answers below show one of each.

ver-001

Passage: The Riverside Café opened in 2015 and quickly became popular with local office workers. It serves breakfast and lunch, but closes at 3pm each day. All of its coffee beans are sourced from a single farm in Colombia. Statement: The Riverside Café serves dinner.

  • ATrue
  • BFalse✓ correct
  • CCannot Say
Why False. The passage states the café serves breakfast and lunch and closes at 3pm. Dinner service is therefore contradicted by the passage.
ver-003

Passage: TechCorp launched its flagship app in March. The company employs 300 people, half of whom work in software development. The remaining staff are split across sales, support, and administration. Statement: TechCorp employs 150 people in software development.

  • ATrue✓ correct
  • BFalse
  • CCannot Say
Why True. Half of 300 is 150, and the passage states half work in software development — the statement follows directly.
ver-008

Passage: The town of Ashford introduced a new recycling scheme in 2020. Within two years, the amount of waste sent to landfill had fallen by 40%. The scheme collects paper, glass and plastic, but not garden waste. Residents who do not sort their recycling correctly receive a written warning. Statement: Residents who repeatedly fail to sort their recycling are fined.

  • ATrue
  • BFalse
  • CCannot Say✓ correct
Why Cannot Say. The passage mentions only a written warning; it says nothing about fines for repeat offenders, so this cannot be determined.
Logical

Logical reasoning

Sequences, codes, deduction, and odd-one-out — pattern recognition with a single correct answer.

log-001

What number continues the sequence? 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

  • A36
  • B40
  • C42✓ correct
  • D44
Why C. The differences grow by 2 each step: +4, +6, +8, +10, so the next gap is +12. 30 + 12 = 42.
log-003

All Bloops are Razzies. All Razzies are Lazzies. Which statement must be true?

  • AAll Bloops are Lazzies✓ correct
  • BAll Lazzies are Bloops
  • CSome Lazzies are not Bloops
  • DNo Bloops are Lazzies
Why A. Bloops ⊆ Razzies ⊆ Lazzies, so every Bloop is a Lazzie. The other options make claims the premises do not guarantee.
log-009

In a code, each letter is replaced by its position in the alphabet (A = 1, B = 2, and so on). If CAT is written as 3-1-20, how is DOG written?

  • A4-15-7✓ correct
  • B4-14-7
  • C3-15-7
  • D4-15-8
Why A. D is the 4th letter, O the 15th, G the 7th, giving 4-15-7.
log-016

Which is the odd one out? Triangle, Square, Circle, Pentagon

  • ATriangle
  • BSquare
  • CCircle✓ correct
  • DPentagon
Why C. A triangle, square and pentagon are all made of straight sides and corners; a circle has neither.
